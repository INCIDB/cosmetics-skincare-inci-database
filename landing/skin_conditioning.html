Skin-Conditioning & Emollient Agents

Skin- and hair-conditioning agents in the sub-functions CosIng records — emollients, humectants, skin-protecting occlusives, soothing and refreshing agents — together with the hair-fixing, antistatic, deodorant, tonic, astringent and oral-care functions. Where an authored comedogenic grade (Fulton 1989) exists, the monograph shows it. 211 ingredients from the free INCIDB sample whose recorded CosIng functions place them here, 173 of them with a CAS number in CosIng. 5 carry a CosIng restriction; 9 have an authored comedogenic grade. Membership is read from the data, not assigned by hand — an ingredient with no CosIng function has no monograph, because there would be nothing to say about it.
