DEHYDROACETIC ACID is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional category Preservative, with CAS number 520-45-6 / 771-03-9 / 16807-48-0 and EC number 208-293-9 / 212-227-4. CosIng also records it as 3-Acetyl-6-methyl-2H-pyran-2,4(3H)-dione (chemical name). CosIng records a restriction against it (V/13), so check the corresponding Annex entry before formulating.
