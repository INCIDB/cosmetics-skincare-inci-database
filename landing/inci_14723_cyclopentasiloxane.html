CYCLOPENTASILOXANE

Not on the EU Annex III list

Cyclopentasiloxane is the cyclic dimethyl polysiloxane that conforms to the formula:

CYCLOPENTASILOXANE is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional categories Skin Conditioning - Emollient, Hair Conditioning, Skin Conditioning, Solvent, with CAS number 541-02-6 and EC number 208-764-9. CosIng records a restriction against it (Restricted under COMMISSION REGULATION (EU) 2024/1328. 1. Shall not be placed on the market (a) as a substance on its own; (b) as a constituent of other substances; or (c) in mixtures; in a concentration equal to or greater than 0,1 % by weight of the respective substance after 6 June 2026. [...] 3. By way of derogation: (a) for D4 and D5 in wash-off cosmetic products, paragraph 1, point (c), shall apply after 31 January 2020. For the purposes of this point, “wash-off cosmetic products” means cosmetic products as defined in Article 2(1), point (a), of Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 of the European Parliament and of the Council (*1) that, under normal conditions of use, are washed off with water after application; (b) for all cosmetic products other than the ones mentioned in paragraph 3(a), paragraph 1 shall apply after 6 June 2027.), so check the corresponding Annex entry before formulating. In the free INCIDB sample it appears on 7 product labels, reaching position 2 on Urban Care Argan Yağı & Keratin Sıvı Saç Bakım Kremi.
