CHONDRUS CRISPUS

Chondrus Crispus is the material obtained from the whole alga, Chondrus crispus, Gigartinacea.

CHONDRUS CRISPUS is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional categories Fragrance, Skin Conditioning. CosIng records no CAS number for it, so the column is NULL rather than guessed. CosIng also records it as Irish Moss (chemical name). In the free INCIDB sample it appears on 2 product labels, reaching position 11 on Crème De Douche Lait D'ânesse Camomille Sans Sulfates.
