Fragrance, Perfuming & Colorant Agents

Fragrance and perfuming ingredients, colorants identified by their Colour Index number (the colorants permitted in EU cosmetics are listed in Annex IV), hair dyes, flavourings and denaturants. Fragrance allergens that must be named on the label are flagged from Annex III on each monograph. 200 ingredients from the free INCIDB sample whose recorded CosIng functions place them here, 192 of them with a CAS number in CosIng. 88 carry a CosIng restriction; 45 are Annex III fragrance allergens; 22 have an authored comedogenic grade. Membership is read from the data, not assigned by hand — an ingredient with no CosIng function has no monograph, because there would be nothing to say about it.
