STEARIC ACID

Rule-flagged fungal-acne trigger

STEARIC ACID is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional categories Cleansing, Surfactant - Emulsifying, Emulsion Stabilising, Fragrance, Refatting, Surfactant - Cleansing, with CAS number 57-11-4 and EC number 200-313-4. In the free INCIDB sample it appears on 18 product labels, reaching position 2 on Nature In A Jar Gentle Detoxifying Cleanser With Agave. INCIDB's rule-derived heuristic flags it as a possible Malassezia (fungal-acne) trigger. That is a rule, not a measurement — treat it as a filter rather than a finding.
