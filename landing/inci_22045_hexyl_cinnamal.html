HEXYL CINNAMAL

EU Annex III fragrance allergen

HEXYL CINNAMAL is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional category Perfuming, with CAS number 101-86-0 and EC number 202-983-3/639-566-4. CosIng records a restriction against it (III/87), so check the corresponding Annex entry before formulating. In the free INCIDB sample it appears on 27 product labels across 8 categories (Cleanser, Deodorant, Haircare, Makeup, Mask, Moisturizer, Serum, Skincare), leading the declaration on Essence Ultîme Elastin Volume Shampooing. It is on the EU Annex III fragrance-allergen list (Regulation (EC) 1223/2009 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/1545), so labelling rules apply above the regulated thresholds.
