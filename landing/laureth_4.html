LAURETH-4

3,6,9,12-tetraoxatetracosan-1-ol; Dodecan-1-ol, ethoxylated; Alcohols, C12-14, ethoxylated

LAURETH-4 is listed in the European Commission CosIng inventory under the functional categories Antistatic, Surfactant - Emulsifying, Fragrance, Surfactant - Cleansing, with CAS number 5274-68-0 / 9002-92-0 / 68439-50-9 and EC number 226-097-1 / 500-002-6 / 500-213-3. In the free INCIDB sample it appears on 6 product labels, reaching position 5 on Vanilla. Fulton (1989) grades it 5 on the 0–5 comedogenicity scale; that grade is transcribed as-is and is the only rating source used.
