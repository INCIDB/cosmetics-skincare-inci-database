The most common cosmetic ingredients

61.2% of products list Aqua / Water, 45.8% list PARFUM and 45.4% list GLYCERIN. The catalogue holds 46,973 distinct canonical INCI names across 318,758 ingredient positions.

Fragrance, preservatives, surfactants and silicones

51.7% of products contain fragrance and 43.9% contain at least one EU-listed fragrance allergen. Parabens appear in 5.5% of products and phenoxyethanol, the preservative that has largely replaced them, in 18.7%.

How common the headline skincare actives are

Vitamin E (tocopherol or tocopheryl acetate) is in 22.9% of products. The actives that drive skincare marketing are rare across the whole catalogue: hyaluronic acid appears in 5.5%, niacinamide in 3.4% and retinoids in 1.1%.

How long ingredient lists are

The median product lists 17 ingredients (mean 17.2, longest 154). facial creams have the longest lists at a median of 26, cleansers the shortest at 13.

What comes first on the label

Ingredients are listed in descending order of concentration, so the first name is the bulk of the product. AQUA leads 39.3% of labels and WATER another 7.9%; ALCOHOL DENAT. tops 2.2%, BUTANE (aerosol propellant) 1.7%.

Regulated ingredients

63.9% of products contain at least one ingredient listed in Annex III of the EU Cosmetics Regulation, the list of substances permitted only under conditions such as a maximum concentration or a mandatory warning. A high share is expected, because Annex III covers everyday preservatives and fragrance components, led here by LIMONENE (24.8%) and LINALOOL (24.4%).

Annex III membership comes from the European Commission CosIng inventory, joined on exact canonical name. It records that a substance is regulated, not whether a given product complies, which depends on concentration and use that labels do not state. Annex II (prohibited substances) is deliberately not summarised here: exact-name joins on that list produce matches that need case-by-case review before they mean anything.

What the ingredients are for

Weighted by how often they appear on labels, 27.8% of ingredient positions carry the CosIng function Fragrance, 23.9% Skin Conditioning and 21.7% Viscosity Controlling.

CosIng assigns one or more functions to each inventory entry. Each ingredient position on a label counts once per function of its ingredient, so shares sum to more than 100%. Denominator: the 259,803 of 318,758 positions whose ingredient has a CosIng function.

How much of the catalogue is enriched, stated two ways

Only 11.0% of the 46,973 distinct canonical names match the CosIng inventory, yet those names fill 82.5% of all 318,758 ingredient positions on labels. The long tail of unmatched strings (botanical variants, multilingual spellings, marketing tokens) is real but rare on any given label.

Method, reuse and citation

  • Source. The full INCIDB snapshot of 2026-09-06: 18,583 product labels from Open Beauty Facts (product data © Open Beauty Facts contributors, ODbL v1.0), ingredient lists parsed into ordered positions and resolved to canonical INCI names, with functions, CAS numbers and Annex membership joined from the European Commission CosIng inventory (contains data from the European Commission CosIng database).
  • Nothing is inferred. A figure counts what labels state. Concentrations are not on labels and are never estimated; where a source has no value the field is NULL and excluded from that figure's denominator, which every section states.
  • The corpus is what contributors scanned. Open Beauty Facts over-represents European mass-market products and the categories its contributors photograph. Shares describe this corpus, not the world market.
  • Refresh. INCIDB ships editions; this page and its charts are regenerated with each edition, so figures move. Cite the snapshot date.
  • Reuse. The figures and charts on this page are published under CC BY 4.0: use them in articles, slides and posts with a link to https://incidb.dataengineered.io/stats/ and the Open Beauty Facts attribution above. The machine-readable version is data.json. The row-level tables are INCIDB Complete; a free 200-product sample is in the public repository.
