EU fragrance allergens: the labelling list

The 81 fragrance-allergen entries of Annex III to the EU Cosmetics Regulation (Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2023/1545). Each one must be named in a cosmetic's ingredient list when its concentration exceeds 0.001 % in leave-on products or 0.01 % in rinse-off products. Next to each entry: how many of the 18,583 product labels in the INCIDB corpus declare it.

  • Entries in the list81
  • Entries found on labels77
  • Products declaring one46.9%
  • Products18,583
  • Snapshot2026-09-25

What the rule requires

Regulation (EU) 2023/1545 rewrote 17 existing Annex III entries and added 45 new ones (entries 327 to 371). Products that do not comply with the amended entries could be placed on the EU market until 31 Jul 2026; that date has passed. Products already on the market may be made available until 31 Jul 2028. For the 13 entries marked *, that transition applies only to products that met the rules in force on 15 August 2023.

Some entries prescribe a collective label name (for example Rose Ketones or Lemongrass Oil), shown in the Label name column. A dash in the Transition column means the consolidated text sets no transition period for that entry. The legal text is the consolidated Regulation on EUR-Lex (version 18.05.2026); this page transcribes it and is not legal advice.

The 81 entries, by how many products declare them

46.9% of the 18,583 products in the corpus declare at least one of these allergens, and 77 of the 81 entries appear on at least one label. About 2.7% of products list their ingredients as unsplit text that the counts below cannot reach.

A count of 0 means no label token INCIDB could resolve matched the entry, not that the substance is absent from the market. Monograph links appear only where the ingredient's own name is printed in the Regulation.
